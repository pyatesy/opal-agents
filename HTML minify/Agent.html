CRO-2936: ILS: List view optimization

To determine if a list view for offer tiles improves customer experience and increases order conversion for ILS renewals.

Inconclusive
3rd September 2025 - 19th September 2025•14 days

Result

-9.42% Renewal Orders

Recommended Action

Iterate

Problem Statement

EOC/OOC customers struggle with unclear ILS renewal page layout. Testing new offer layout with primary offer at top and bigger tiles to improve clarity and engagement.

Key Findings

  • Overall orders significantly dropped (-10% total, -9% renewal, -16% customise), all statistically significant.
  • EOC customers saw larger renewal order decreases (-10.2%) compared to OOC customers (-8.2%).
  • Conversion from view cart to order significantly dropped (-10.72% OOC, -13.8% EOC) on review page.

Next Steps & Recommendations

  • Iterate on V1, leveraging positive engagement and progression to review page.
  • Split experience by EOC/OOC, integrating previous winning review page experiment.
  • Improve imagery with more engaging visuals, possibly animation, instead of hardware.
